Help when you need it

Support

Most issues can be resolved without resetting or deleting your data. Start with the safe checks below, or contact us with enough detail to investigate.

Email support

ytstats@extensions.yashkhandelwal.me

Privacy questions

See exactly what is handled in the Privacy Policy.

Tracking appears to have stopped

  1. Keep the YouTube tab open and reload that tab once.
  2. Open the extension popup and confirm that its statistics load normally.
  3. In chrome://extensions, confirm YouTube Watch Stats is enabled.
  4. Avoid clearing data or reinstalling before exporting a backup from Full Stats.

Continue Watching does not appear

Continue Watching applies to eligible unfinished regular videos. It intentionally avoids very early positions, nearly completed videos, and Shorts. Confirm the feature is enabled in the Full Stats actions menu.

Before importing a backup

Keep the original JSON file. The extension validates it and shows a summary before making changes. Choose Merge to add compatible history while avoiding duplicates, or Overwrite only when you intentionally want to replace tracking data.

Report a problem

Please include:

  • Your extension version, shown in chrome://extensions
  • Your browser name and version
  • Whether the content was a regular video or a Short
  • The exact sequence that caused the issue
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • A screenshot with personal account information removed, if useful
Please protect your data

Do not email exported history files, passwords, cookies, account credentials, or private browsing information unless support specifically explains why a narrowly scoped sample is required.

Data controls

Full Stats provides export, import, Reset Today, individual deletion, and full deletion controls. Read each confirmation carefully because their scopes differ. Export a backup before a destructive operation when the history matters to you.
