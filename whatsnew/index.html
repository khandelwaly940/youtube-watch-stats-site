v2.0A major upgrade to how you explore your stats⌄
  • Date Range Selector: Filter your stats by 30 days, 90 days, 6 months, 12 months, all time, or any custom date range you choose.
  • Unified View: Everything—summary cards, chart, top videos, channels, and watch history—updates together when you change the range.
  • Redesigned Chart: Switch between bar and line views with a single click. The chart adapts automatically—daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly—based on the range.
  • Video Thumbnails: Every video and short now shows its thumbnail across your stats page and popup search.
  • Unified History: Videos and shorts are now in one place. Use the filter toggle to switch between All, Videos, or Shorts.
  • Improved Import: A clean selection screen replaces the old text prompt. Choose Merge or Overwrite with one tap.
  • Smarter Merge: Re-importing the same backup file no longer doubles your stats.
v1.5Shorts tracking fix⌄
  • YouTube changed their internal page structure, which broke Shorts tracking. This update fixes it.
v1.4Bug fixes & performance⌄
  • Seek Time Fix: Fixed a bug where seeking forward would incorrectly add watch time.
  • Performance: Optimized tracking logic for better accuracy.
v1.3New features & improvements⌄
  • Date-wise History: Video history grouped by date with timestamps.
  • Top Channels: Top 10 channels by watch time.
  • Top Videos Filter: Top videos from the last 30 days.
  • Dynamic Stats: Accurate totals for Active/Archive views.
v1.2Shorts support & archive⌄
  • Shorts Tracking: Now tracks YouTube Shorts separately!
  • History: Separate history lists for Videos and Shorts.
  • Archive: Videos older than 1 year are automatically archived.
